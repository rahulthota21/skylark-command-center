# 90-second evaluator demo script

1. Open the public Skylark Command Center URL. Point out the **Live monday.com data** badge, board record counts, and last-sync timestamp.
2. Ask: **“How is our pipeline looking for the energy sector this quarter?”**
3. Point out the executive headline, verified metric cards, stage/sector chart, assumptions, data-quality caveats, and expandable source records that link back to monday.com.
4. Ask: **“Which work orders are delayed or at risk?”** Show that delivery health is handled separately and that value is not incorrectly labelled as realized revenue.
5. Ask: **“How are billing, collections, and receivables looking?”** Show consistent inclusive-of-GST financial metrics, source records, and that Amount Receivable is not recomputed or invented.
6. Click **Generate update**. Show the Leadership Update sections and Copy Markdown button.
7. In monday.com, change one deal value/stage or work-order status. Return to the app and click **Refresh**. Show the timestamp and result changing.
8. Finish with: “The app does not use a runtime spreadsheet fallback. Numbers are calculated in code; AI only interprets the question and communicates evidence-backed results.”

## Good evaluator test questions

- How is our pipeline looking this quarter?
- Compare sector performance across the pipeline.
- Which deals need executive attention?
- Which work orders are delayed or at risk?
- Which accounts have active deals and delivery risk?
- Prepare a leadership update.
- What assumptions did the agent make?
